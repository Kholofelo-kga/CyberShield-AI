About CyberShield AI

CyberShield AI is a research prototype designed to investigate the use of Natural Language Processing and traditional machine-learning techniques for automated cyberbullying detection in social-media text.

The system analyses textual content and assigns one of six classifications while presenting class probabilities, confidence information and recommendations for human review where uncertainty is detected.
